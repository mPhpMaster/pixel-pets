Pixel Pets 🐾

Move your mouse around, and your pet will follow it.

  1. Click the pet to open its menu.
  2. Pick from 3 tabs: 23 animals, 11 chibi anime characters, or 6 classic characters (pirate, astronaut, ghost…). Then choose a color, size and speed.
  3. Try ❤️ Pet and 🍪 Treat, or add more pets.
  4. Drag & throw a pet. It flies, bounces and lands dizzy!
  5. With 2+ pets, leave the mouse still and watch them play together.
  6. Switch the language to العربية to make them talk in Arabic.
  7. Scroll down, then set On scroll to 📌 Stay on screen or 📜 Move with page.
