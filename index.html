Pixel Pets 🐾

Move your mouse around, and your pet will follow it.

  1. Click the pet to open its menu.
  2. Pick one of 17 animals, a color, size and speed.
  3. Try ❤️ Pet and 🍪 Treat, or add more pets.
  4. Switch the language to العربية to make them talk in Arabic.
